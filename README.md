# Whiskey Business Research Ledger

**Open the ledger: https://profangrybeard.github.io/WhiskeyBusiness-ResearchLedger/**

Version 0.1.2 · Updated October 28, 2026

The sources behind Whiskey Business, a game about Prohibition-era moonshine in Carroll County, Virginia, by Tim Lindsey. Each source is one numbered entry with an MLA 9 citation, a trust grade, figures, open leads, and the claims still to verify.

The primary research question: how we make a player feel OUTLAW, and secondarily PRIDE in the product, as the player balances fame, infamy, and community.

## AI use

This ledger is made with Claude (Anthropic, Opus 5.5), documented in the AI 201 ESF format until SCAD sets a policy.

- **Claude wrote:** the page code and design, the source summaries, the citation formatting, the `bears_on` tags, the filing rules, and the nightly filing run.
- **Tim directed and decided:** what the ledger is for, the research question, the pillar words, the citation style, what to keep, change or reject, and every design call.
- **Tim's alone:** the use lines, design links, research questions, reading notes, and every why line in the AI log. Claude never writes them.
- **Checked or not:** every summary is marked unchecked until Tim has read the original. An unchecked summary is a lead, not evidence.
- **The record:** the AI log tab on the page (`ai-log.json`) holds the Direction Log and Records of Resistance. Commits Claude makes carry a Co-Authored-By line, and their messages say why.

This ledger stands apart from any edition of the game. Mash Edition is the first.
